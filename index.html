Where Clinical Expertise Meets Prophetic Calling

Boladale Gold-Okelanfaa is a licensed clinician, prophetic voice, author, and healing ministry leader whose life's work sits at the sacred intersection of psychology and faith. Her journey began in 2009 at Adventist Behavioral Health, working with juveniles in residential care — where she first witnessed the truth that would define her entire career: untreated parental pain does not stay with the parent.

Completing her master's in counseling psychology while immersed in direct client care, Boladale developed a rare integration of clinical precision and spiritual authority. She saw mothers who believed God was punishing them through their children's pain — and she learned to redirect shame into love, breaking cycles that had run for generations.

Today, operating as Prophetess Boladale Gold-Okelanfaa, she serves individuals across trauma, mood, anxiety, relationships, and occupational stress — through her private practice, her international healing ministry, her four-book Amazon series, and the Moving Beyond healing platform. Her approach is eclectic, somatic, bilateral, and uncompromisingly faith-grounded.

The people of God cannot thrive when they cannot move beyond what broke them. That conviction is the heartbeat of everything she does.
